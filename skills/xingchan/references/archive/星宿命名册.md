## 一、主名卡

**星躔** ｜ 拼音 **Xingchán** ｜ 英文 **StellarTrack** ｜ 图腾：星行于天，文成于迹

- EN: *StellarTrack* — an academic writing pipeline mapped onto the Chinese Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions. “躔” (chán) is the classical astronomical term for a star's traced path across the sky; a paper's argument, likewise, is a track the author lays down across the mansions. Heaven's pattern becomes the text's pattern.
- 中文：躔，星辰运行之轨迹度次（《方言》“日运为躔”，《汉书·律历志》“日月初躔”）。文章章法如星之躔度——写作即顺天行文，以星宿为骨、以文迹为轨。天文与人文，同构于一“躔”。
